What both of their Exits missed

  • Assessment and recognizing cues, evaluating outcomes, basic care and comfort, renal, peds, psych, trauma, vital signs, math.

Endurance plan

  • Week by week: 50 → 75 → 100, then a 150 long sit. One sit a week, on a day off.
  • Pace is about 80 seconds a question. Take a 2-minute stand-up break every 50, like a long shift.
  • Get at least one full 100 done before the term starts.
