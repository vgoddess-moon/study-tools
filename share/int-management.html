Management / Leadership HESI Prep

Priority · delegation · legal · ethics · safety · disaster · 147 questions (81 new + 66 med-surg priority)

This is the RN Management HESI: its own 50-question test, about 60 minutes. The prior cohort’s report tagged 41 of 50 as Leadership and 24 as Legal/Ethical, and half were priority calls about med-surg clients. So this page drills the decision rules, not the leadership textbook.

The Ladder: where this page sits

What the real test looked like

  • 50 scored questions, 60 minutes, 4 NGN standalones (bowtie, trend items).
  • The student who passed (954) lost points on taking action (10 of 19), health care law (3 of 6) and documentation (0 of 2).
  • The book did not match because the test is NCLEX judgment: who first, who can do it, is it legal.

The rules that decide most questions

  • Who first: airway, breathing, circulation; unstable over stable; acute over chronic; unexpected over expected.
  • Delegation: the RN keeps assessment, teaching, evaluation and the unstable client. UAP gets the routine and predictable. LPN gets stable clients and reinforces teaching.
  • Transfer, discharge, float nurse, disaster: pick the most stable client.
  • Stem already gives the abnormal number? Act. Don’t assess again.

Where the questions come from

  • 81 new questions written for this test’s categories, NCLEX style, rationales on every option.
  • Med-Surg Priority = the 66 judgment questions from the Med-Surg HESI by Topic bank (study-guide keyed), each with its priority-rule line.
