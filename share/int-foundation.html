The Ladder: where this page sits

Why this comes first

  • The other cohort’s reports leaked the same basics on both Exits: vital signs, physical assessment, math, basic care. Those are recall, not judgment.
  • When the words and numbers are automatic, your brain is free for the actual question. That is what builds endurance.

How to use it

  • 15–20 minutes a day, one section or Mixed 50. Work days count; do it from bed.
  • Labs: learn the number and what high or low does to the client. The “What the number means” questions train that.
  • Drug endings: the ending tells you the class, and the class tells you the side effects.

Where the content comes from

  • Standard nursing terminology and pharmacology classes.
  • Lab ranges are the adult ranges in the Ignatavicius med-surg text. Where books differ (magnesium, creatinine by sex), the hint says so.
