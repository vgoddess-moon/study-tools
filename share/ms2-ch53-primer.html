Ch 53 — Assessment of the Endocrine System

MS2 Exam 5 · built from Prof. Vil’s recap sheet

Chapter 53 is her gland-by-gland map — location, hormones, conditions. It is the chapter everything else in the hormone block stands on. The single highest-yield idea in it is the primary vs. secondary rule: when the stimulating hormone is HIGH and the target hormone is LOW, the gland itself failed. When both are LOW, the pituitary failed — or, far more commonly, someone stopped their steroids. Two corrections to carry forward: calcitonin is not the active form of vitamin D (that is calcitriol — calcitonin comes from thyroid C cells and LOWERS calcium), and the posterior pituitary manufactures nothing.
