How an Unfolding Case Works

What this is

  • Unfolding case studies in the HESI style: one client, the story moves forward, and each question adds new data (labs, a new symptom, a call from home).
  • Source: HESI-style case studies, rewritten in our own words with the keyed answers kept. Where a HESI key teaches an older idea (allopurinol "dissolves" stones, the shellfish/iodine link), the explanation says what is actually true.
  • 208 Med-Surg questions across 10 cases, plus a peds leukemia case as a bonus.

How to use it

  • One case a day. Questions stay in story order, so start at the top and read every blue Case update box.
  • Before each answer: name the cue, the rule, and why the runner-up loses.
  • 🧭 Priority & First Action (61 questions) pulls the judgment questions from every case into one shuffled drill.
  • Green "Priority rule" lines appear on the judgment questions.
