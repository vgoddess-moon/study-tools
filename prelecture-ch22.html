Before you start — the big picture

Chapter 22 is about how nurses assess breathing. Two priority concepts run through the whole chapter:

Gas exchange — getting oxygen into the blood and carbon dioxide out (ventilation + diffusion at the alveoli).
Perfusion — blood actually flowing through the lung tissue to pick up that oxygen.
Key words you'll meet: atelectasis (alveoli collapse), hypoxemia (low oxygen in the blood), hypoxia (low oxygen in the tissues), crepitus (air trapped under the skin, crackly feel), fremitus (vibration felt on the chest when the patient speaks), and adventitious sounds (abnormal lung sounds: crackles, wheezes, rhonchi, pleural friction rub).
