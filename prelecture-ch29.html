Before you start — the big picture

Chapter 29 is diseases of the heart itself — failure, valves, infection, muscle.

Left-sided HF → blood backs up into the LUNGS (crackles, shortness of breath, pulmonary edema). Right-sided HF → blood backs up into the BODY (JVD, peripheral edema, weight gain).
Systolic HF = can't CONTRACT well. Diastolic HF = can't RELAX/fill well. BNP = blood lab that rises in heart failure (the heart\'s "stress alarm").
Valve disease: stenosis = stiff/narrowed; regurgitation = leaky/backflow. Infective endocarditis = infection of the heart lining/valves.
Cardiac tamponade = fluid compresses the heart → emergency. Cardiomyopathy = diseased heart muscle (dilated, hypertrophic, restrictive).
