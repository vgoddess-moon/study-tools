Before you start — the big picture

Chapter 26 is the critical-care end of the respiratory unit — the true emergencies.

Pulmonary embolism (PE): a clot blocks lung blood flow. Sudden dyspnea, sharp chest pain, anxiety. Treated with anticoagulants + bleeding precautions.
Acute respiratory failure (ARF): PaO₂ < 60 (oxygenation failure) OR PaCO₂ > 50 with pH < 7.35 (ventilation failure).
ARDS: refractory hypoxemia (low O₂ that won't fix even on 100% oxygen); "ground-glass" lungs on x-ray; often triggered by sepsis/trauma.
Ventilator alarms: HIGH-pressure = obstruction (secretions, mucus plug, biting tube); LOW-pressure = leak/disconnection. Tension pneumothorax → needle decompression.
