Before you start — the big picture

Chapter 27 is how nurses assess the heart and vessels. Learn these core terms now — the whole unit leans on them.

Cardiac output (CO) = Heart Rate × Stroke Volume (blood pumped per minute). Ejection fraction (EF) = % of blood the left ventricle ejects per beat.
Preload = how much the heart stretches as it FILLS (end of diastole). Afterload = the resistance it must push against to EJECT.
Systole = contraction/emptying. Diastole = relaxation/filling.
Troponin (T & I) = lab markers released when heart muscle is injured. Cardiac catheterization = the definitive invasive test.
