What these two files are. The med-surg bank is a Brunner & Suddarth test bank (1,385 pages, 73 chapters) — a totally different textbook from your Ignatavicius. Its chapter numbers do not line up with yours, which is why "Ch 61" in that file is dermatology, not reproductive. The maternity bank is Leifer 9th edition (318 pages, 34 chapters) — same book as yours, one edition back, and the early chapters got renumbered in the 10th.
Use these as a question-shape drill, not as truth. Rationales in an older/different edition can be out of date. If a question here contradicts your cookbook or the professor's slides, your cookbook wins. Page numbers below are PDF page numbers — type them into your reader's "go to page" box.
