Before you start — the big picture

Chapter 32 is heart attacks — what happens when a coronary artery gets blocked.

ACS (acute coronary syndrome) = umbrella for unstable angina + MI, from a ruptured plaque forming a clot in a coronary artery.
Stable angina = predictable chest pain, relieved by rest/nitroglycerin. Unstable angina = pain at rest, not easily relieved (a warning of impending MI).
Ischemia = oxygen-starved heart muscle (reversible). Infarction = actual muscle death (necrosis). Troponin rises with infarction.
STEMI = 100% blockage, ST elevation, true emergency. NSTEMI = partial, no ST elevation. PCI = balloon/stent (ideally within 90 min). CABG = bypass surgery.
