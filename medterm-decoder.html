The habit this trains — slash before you Google:
When you hit a scary word, put a slash in it first and guess. hepat / o / megaly → liver + enlargement → enlarged liver. Reaching for the answer before you see it is what makes it stick. And bridge from words you already know: you know hepatitis → so hepat = liver.
