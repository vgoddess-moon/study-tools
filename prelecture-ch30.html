Before you start — the big picture

Chapter 30 is blood-vessel disease. The master skill: telling arterial from venous problems.

PAD (arterial): not enough oxygen-rich blood reaching the limb → pale, cool, hairless skin, poor pulses, painful "arterial" ulcers. Classic symptom: intermittent claudication (leg pain with walking, relieved by rest).
Venous insufficiency: blood can\'t get back to the heart → edema, brownish skin (hyperpigmentation), irregular stasis ulcers around the ankles.
ABI (ankle-brachial index) = ankle BP ÷ arm BP; screens for arterial disease.
Aneurysm = bulging weak artery; aortic dissection = a tear (highly lethal). VTE risk = Virchow\'s triad: stasis, vessel injury, hypercoagulability.
