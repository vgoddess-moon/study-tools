Before you start — the big picture

Chapter 31 is shock — widespread failure to deliver oxygen to the cells.

Shock = oxygen/perfusion needs not met → tissue hypoxia → cells fail. Watch MAP (needs ≥ 60–70 to perfuse organs).
Stages: Initial → Compensatory (body shunts blood to vital organs) → Progressive (compensation fails) → Refractory (irreversible damage, death).
Types: Hypovolemic (blood/fluid loss), Cardiogenic (pump fails), Distributive (massive vasodilation: anaphylactic, neurogenic, septic), Obstructive (something blocks flow).
Sepsis/septic shock = dysregulated response to infection → circulatory collapse. MODS = 2+ organ systems failing together.
