Before you start — the big picture

Chapter 28 is heart rhythms. You already have the EKG foundation — this names the rhythms.

ECG waves: P = atrial depolarization, QRS = ventricular depolarization, T = ventricular repolarization.
NSR (normal sinus rhythm) = from the SA node, 60–100 bpm. A-fib = atria quiver, no clear P waves, loss of "atrial kick," high clot/stroke risk.
V-fib = chaotic ventricles, NO pulse → CPR + defibrillation. PACs/PVCs = early extra beats from irritable spots.
Defibrillation = unsynchronized shock for lethal rhythms; cardioversion = synchronized shock to reset organized rhythms.
