Before you start — the big picture

Chapter 24 is chronic lower-airway disease. The two headliners are asthma and COPD.

Asthma = chronic but reversible, intermittent airway narrowing. Managed with control drugs (daily anti-inflammatories to prevent) + reliever drugs (fast bronchodilators to stop an attack).
COPD = chronic bronchitis (inflammation) + emphysema (loss of alveolar elasticity). Largely irreversible. Hallmark sign: barrel chest (1:1 chest diameter).
Cor pulmonale = right-sided heart failure caused by lung disease. Cystic fibrosis = inherited (autosomal recessive) thick-mucus disease.
Chest tube tip: bubbling in the water-seal chamber = an air leak.
