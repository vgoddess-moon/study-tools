Before you start — the big picture

Chapter 25 is the infectious lung chapter. Four big players: influenza, COVID-19, pneumonia, and tuberculosis (TB).

Influenza: antivirals work best within 24–48 hours of symptoms; prevent with annual vaccine + handwashing.
Pneumonia: in older adults the first sign is often acute confusion (hypoxia), not fever. Treat fast to prevent sepsis.
TB: spread by airborne droplets; signs include night sweats, weight loss, low-grade fever, hemoptysis. Treated with months of multi-drug therapy, often by DOT (Directly Observed Therapy). Avoid alcohol (TB drugs stress the liver).
Vocab: anosmia (loss of smell), ageusia (loss of taste), empyema (pus in the pleural space).
