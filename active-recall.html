How to use this app

Active recall workflow:

  • Read the prompt. Try to answer in your head, type it in Keyboard mode, or write it on the canvas with your Apple Pencil in Pencil mode.
  • Stuck? Tap Hint for a small clue.
  • Check yourself progressively: Tap Show Keywords to see key terms, then Show Summary for a condensed version, then Show Full Answer for the complete answer.
  • Self-grade honestly. Tap Got it or Missed it. The app tracks what you actually know.

Smart features:

  • Keyboard / Pencil toggle — switch between typing and handwriting on each card. Your preference is saved.
  • Progressive reveal — Keywords, Summary, then Full Answer. Test yourself at each layer.
  • Missed Only mode brings back only cards you missed — focus on weak spots.
  • Shuffle mode randomizes order so you don't pattern-match.
  • Filter chips let you study one exam at a time.
  • Progress auto-saves — close the app and come back later.
  • The 3-dot streak shows how many times in a row you've gotten a card right.

Apple Pencil: Pen tool to write, eraser to fix, trash to clear. Your writing doesn't save between cards — this is intentional, just like a whiteboard.

Active recall tips:

  • Resist peeking. Discomfort = the brain working.
  • Can't answer in 15-20 seconds? Use a hint or reveal. Don't block.
  • Honest grading > feeling good. Mark Missed if you weren't 100% sure.
  • 10 cards twice beats 50 cards once.
